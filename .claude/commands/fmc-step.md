Plan the step named here, or the Status table's next step if none is named: $ARGUMENTS

1. Read `docs/fmc/ROADMAP.md` and `docs/fmc/EXECUTION.md` for that step.
2. Write the plan:
   - the files you will add or change, and on which branch;
   - the tests;
   - what runs in Revit;
   - the risks;
   - the decisions you need from me.
3. Show me the plan and stop. Write no code until I say yes.
4. After my yes, follow "The loop for every step" in EXECUTION.md to the end, including the status update and the report.
